Why this site exists

This site is where I document two things I genuinely enjoy: motorcycle travel and personal technology projects at home.

The goal is simple: share what I’m learning, show how I approach projects, and give future employers, collaborators, and fellow enthusiasts a clear view into my interests, problem-solving style, and technical curiosity.

What you’ll find here

  • Motorcycle trip write-ups with routes, gear notes, and lessons learned
  • Home lab and tech projects with architecture, tools, and troubleshooting notes
  • Consistent post layouts with images and optional video
  • A growing archive of practical experiments and personal builds
