My corner of the internet

I'm Daniel Gonzalez. I work in tech, enjoy building projects at home, and ride motorcycles whenever I get the chance. This site brings those interests together: the things I build, the roads I explore, and what I learn along the way.

On January 6, 2026, I wrote my first post about finally starting my personal website, gonzalezdaniel.net. I'd wanted a place to share my thoughts and projects for a long time, but figuring out where to begin felt daunting.

Learning by doing

NetworkChuck's videos on networking, cybersecurity, and technology helped me get moving. His encouragement to do the labs and build things pushed me to stop putting it off and start learning through practice.

I chose Hostinger for hosting and registered gonzalezdaniel.net. Seeing my own name connected to a little space on the internet made the project feel real. Now I have a place to document my progress and share what I discover.

What I share here

  • Home tech projects, from experiments that sharpen my skills to lessons from troubleshooting.
  • My interests in software development, cybersecurity, networking, and automation.
  • Motorcycle trips, local food stops, scenic roads, and the stories that come with them.

This is an ongoing journey. I hope sharing the process helps other curious people find an idea to try, a useful lesson, or a reason to start something of their own.
